step 1: convert to correct format in handbrake app

Change it to:

Codec: H.264 (x264)
Bit depth: 8-bit (automatic when using H.264)
Resolution:
either keep 4K (2160×3840)
OR downscale to 1080×1920 (recommended for Instagram)
Framerate: Same as source (or 30 fps if you want smaller file)

step 2: some touch ups and edits in davinci resolve

step 2.1: change from portrait to Instagram post mode

1. Change timeline resolution

Go to:
File → Project Settings → Master Settings

Set:

Timeline Resolution → Custom
Width: 1080
Height: 1350

Click Save

2. Fix your dimension in the frame (so you get black bars on sides)

Click your clip → go to Inspector (top right) → video → transofrm

Adjust:

Zoom X and Y: 1.0 → 0.7
black bars on left/right (this is what you want)

🎥 Export settings (for IG post)

In Deliver tab:

Format: MP4
Codec: H.264
Resolution: 1080×1350
FPS: same as source
Quality: Restrict to ~10–20 Mbps
